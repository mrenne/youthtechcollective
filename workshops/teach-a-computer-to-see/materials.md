# Teach a Computer to See — Materials List

Prices were checked in October 2026 unless marked **est.** (estimate). Retail prices change often, so confirm them before you buy. The core workshop needs no microcontrollers or electronics, so it's inexpensive to run.

## Ask the partner to provide

| Item | Notes |
|---|---|
| Laptops or Chromebooks with built-in webcams, 1 per pair (8 for 16 teens) | **Test them ahead of time** (see the facilitator guide). Managed Chromebooks sometimes block the camera or certain websites. |
| Chrome or Edge browser, up to date | Teachable Machine works best in Chrome. |
| Wi-Fi | Needed only to load the page. Training runs in the browser. |
| Projector or large screen with HDMI | For live demos. |
| Tables with power outlets | Training uses a lot of battery. |
| Whiteboard or flip chart | Nice to have. |

## Facilitator brings

| Item | Use in this workshop |
|---|---|
| Facilitator laptop | Demo machine and backup station |
| Optional: micro:bit V2 boards (2–4) with battery packs and USB cables | Fast-finisher level-up with micro:bit CreateAI (motion-based AI). In radio mode, each setup needs **2 boards**: one to collect data and one plugged in as a bridge. V1 boards can collect and train but can't run models. US retailers list micro:bit V2 Go kits at roughly **$25–40 each** ([Walmart](https://www.walmart.com/c/kp/microbit), [Reach.dog/Vilros](https://m.reach.dog/shop/vilroscom/products/micro-bit-v2-go-bundle)). |
